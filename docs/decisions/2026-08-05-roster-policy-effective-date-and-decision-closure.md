# Roster Policy Resolution Uses Explicit Effective Dates and Closed Decisions

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-05

## Decision

Resolve policy with an explicit evaluation context and inclusive effective windows. Fingerprint only the effective set. Persist generation snapshots. Permit department confirmation only from repository-authored options with explicit authority, dates, and evidence. Present choices and impacts in the department artifact.

## Consequences

Future revisions cannot activate early, expired or rejected revisions cannot remain current, and superseded decisions remain historically explainable. Overlapping effective choices fail or are atomically closed by deliberate supersession. Historical generation is not reinterpreted. Free-text policy identifiers are no longer an operator control.

## Deferred

Real department decisions, advanced fatigue and overtime policy, optimizer changes, publication, workbook inference, and automatic confirmation remain outside this slice.
