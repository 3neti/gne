# Roster Policy Confirmability

`RosterPolicyConfirmability` distinguishes `confirmable`, `configuration_required`, `unsupported_in_current_release`, `unresolved`, and `invalid`. A policy can be confirmed only when its closed registered option is supported, all required typed parameters normalize successfully, and its effective window is valid. Application-service validation is authoritative; form and UI validation are only an earlier convenience.

Unsupported options stay visible with their dependency but cannot become effective. Decision-pending options cannot be confirmed. Ordinary rejection creates no durable business audit. Successful revisions retain normalized configuration, window, authority, evidence, notes, and fingerprint.

Confirmed revisions use permanent supersession. A newer start closes the prior revision on the preceding day. When a bounded newer revision expires, the prior revision does not resume; the repository provisional fallback applies unless another confirmed revision covers the date. Temporary override with automatic restoration is deferred.
