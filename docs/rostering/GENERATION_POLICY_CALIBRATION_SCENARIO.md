# Generation Policy Calibration Scenario

`ANAESTHESIA-GENERATION-POLICY-CALIBRATION` verifies the eight-policy register, visible provisional status, mandatory/quality diagnostics, deterministic equal and proportional examples, unresolved fairness behavior, and policy fingerprint provenance. Operational mutations roll back unless `--keep-state` is explicitly supplied.

The scenario produces a meeting-oriented JSON, seven-page HTML set, and concise PDF. It does not optimize, publish, interpret the legacy workbook, or confirm a department decision automatically.
