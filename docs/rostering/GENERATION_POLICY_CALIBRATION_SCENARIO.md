# Generation Policy Calibration Scenario

`ANAESTHESIA-GENERATION-POLICY-CALIBRATION` verifies the eight-policy register, visible provisional status, mandatory/quality diagnostics, deterministic equal and proportional examples, unresolved fairness behavior, and policy fingerprint provenance. Operational mutations roll back unless `--keep-state` is explicitly supplied.

The twelve-step scenario proves provisional resolution, future exclusion, current activation, expiry, revision ordering, overlap rejection, effective fingerprint transitions, closed options, no-mutation preview, eight pending decisions, candidate-choice rendering, and rollback. It produces JSON, thirteen linked HTML pages, and a thirteen-page PDF. It does not optimize, publish, interpret the legacy workbook, or confirm a department decision automatically.
