# Generation Policy Calibration Artifact Report

Default output: `.gne/reports/rostering/policy-calibration/`.

- `report.json` is the machine-readable policy inventory and comparison proof.
- `html/index.html` links thirteen pages covering current/future policy, ten questionnaire topics, impacts, and confirmation.
- `pdf/anaesthesia-generation-policy-calibration.pdf` is the thirteen-page decision pack with registered candidates, consequences, department selections, notes, authority, effective date, evidence, and signature fields.

Artifacts contain no credentials, absolute developer paths, or doctor identifiers. Checksums and HTML aggregate fingerprint are reported by the scenario command.

The fifteen-page pack includes a confirmability legend, registered parameter fields, disabled unsupported options, discovery-only holiday and credited-hours questions, permanent-supersession warning, impact comparisons, and a final authority record. The renderer consumes the same repository-authored parameter schema as confirmation.
